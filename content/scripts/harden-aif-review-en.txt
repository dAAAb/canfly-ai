OpenClaw agents can call the terminal, the browser, and your files. One bad tool call is enough to leak a secret or wipe the wrong folder.

Harden AIF is a local-first firewall for those tool calls. Install it once with the official script from aif.harden.run. Then pin OpenClaw with aif configure --agent openclaw --validate.

OpenClaw uses a bridge process with block-and-steer. Unsafe covered actions stop before they run, and AIF suggests a safer next step. You review Allow, Block, Ask, and Redact in the local dashboard on port 47391.
